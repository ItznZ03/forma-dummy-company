Career & Business Prospects

Emerging Industry Roles

This tech movement opens up major business opportunities for startups to launch digital tools quickly and cheaply. It also creates valuable new career tracks, including AI Prompt Architects, Product Integration Consultants, and Low-Code Workflow Analysts.

Entrepreneurship & Industry Issues

Successful Entrepreneur Qualities

Strategic Adaptability The tech industry changes fast. Successful leaders must actively update their skills as automated tools improve.
Problem-First Vision Great founders focus on real-world market problems, using AI tools simply as a fast way to build the solution.

Current Issues in ICT

The Automation Skills Gap Over-reliance on automated tools can lower basic programming knowledge among entry-level developers.
Logic & Quality Control AI models can generate errors. Human verification is still required to guarantee security and system accuracy.
