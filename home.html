What is Forma?

Forma is an AI platform built to turn simple text descriptions into fully working software apps. Instead of manually writing thousands of lines of code, users can just explain what they want to build in plain English. By bridging the gap between an idea and a functional product, it speeds up development and allows teams to launch software instantly.
